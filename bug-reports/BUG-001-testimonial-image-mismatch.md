#  BUG-001 — Testimonial Image Does Not Match Testimonial Attribution

![Severity](https://img.shields.io/badge/Severity-Medium-orange)
![Priority](https://img.shields.io/badge/Priority-Medium-yellow)
![Type](https://img.shields.io/badge/Type-Content%20%2F%20UI-blue)

###  Location

**Homepage → REDX testimonial**

https://www.shopup.org/

###  Steps to Reproduce

1. Open https://www.shopup.org/
2. Scroll to the customer testimonial section.
3. Locate the testimonial titled:

   **"REDX saved my business"**

4. Check the testimonial attribution:

   **Rabeya Ahmed**  
   **Beauty Blend**
   
6. Click on right/left arrow button for next testimonial.

7. Compare the attribution with the photograph displayed beside the testimonial.

### Actual Result

The testimonial is attributed to **Rabeya Ahmed / Beauty Blend**, while the displayed photograph appears to show a male customer.

**Emdad Hossain** | **Emdad General Store**

### Expected Result

The image should correspond to the customer identified in the testimonial.

If the image is intended to be a representative/stock image, it should be clearly identified as such instead of appearing to represent the named customer.

### Impact

This can:

- Reduce trust in customer testimonials
- Create confusion about the identity of the customer
- Affect the credibility of the testimonial section
